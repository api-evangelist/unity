Unite Seoul 2026 Keynote announced the release of Unity 7, offering a sneak peek of some of the upcoming features, and major updates, including modern CoreCLR and .NET, 2D and 3D graphics tooling, and a free Unity CLI and MCP server to power better collaboration inside and outside the Unity Editor. The event also previewed updates to the In-App Purchase (IAP) SDK for direct-to-consumer commerce, ad creative enhancements powered by Unity Vector, and new tools for Industry enterprise customers.
